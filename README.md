# Muscle Fitness

**Muscle Fitness is an evidence-aware AI fitness coaching platform that
connects training, nutrition and recovery around one real client
profile.**

Its AI layer, **Dante**, turns that context into practical coaching
instead of returning the same generic answer to every user.

> Dedication. Determination. Drive. Discipline.

> Today is the youngest you will ever be.

---

## The Problem

Fitness information is abundant but fragmented.

Users face:

- generic workout programs
- contradictory nutrition information
- supplement misinformation
- disconnected workout, food and recovery tools
- difficulty adapting programs
- limited access to personalized coaching
