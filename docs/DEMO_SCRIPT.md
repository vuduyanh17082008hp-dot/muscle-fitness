# Muscle Fitness — 3–5 Minute AI Fair Demo

## 00:00–00:25 — The Problem

Open the homepage.

Explain:

“Fitness information is everywhere, but most advice is generic and
fragmented.”

Show the Problem section.

## 00:25–00:50 — Solution

Show:

ONE PROFILE.
ONE SYSTEM.
ONE COACH.

Explain that Muscle Fitness connects:

- Training
- Nutrition
- Recovery
- Dante

## 00:50–01:20 — Client Profile

Login to a demo account.

Show:

- goal
- experience
- training days
- body information
- nutrition preferences

## 01:20–02:05 — Workout Builder

Open:

Dashboard → Workouts

Show:

- recommended split
- multiple split options
- custom split
- priority muscles
- intensity
- volume
- RIR
- exercise selection

## 02:05–02:40 — Nutrition

Show:

- calories
- protein
- carbohydrates
- fats
- food preferences
- meal guidance

If external food data works, show it.

## 02:40–03:45 — Dante

Ask:

“Based on my current goal, training frequency and recovery context,
should I add more chest volume this week or prioritize recovery?”

Then demonstrate a research question such as:

“Is creatine monohydrate useful for my goal? Separate strong evidence
from uncertainty.”

Highlight:

- client context
- external evidence
- uncertainty
- personalized output

## 03:45–04:15 — Responsible AI

Open:

`/responsible-ai`

Explain:

“Dante provides educational fitness and wellness guidance. It does not
diagnose disease or replace a medical professional.”

## 04:15–04:45 — Closing

Return to homepage.

Show:

DEDICATION.
DETERMINATION.
DRIVE.
DISCIPLINE.

Closing:

“Muscle Fitness gives people a more contextual, practical and
responsible starting point for fitness.”
