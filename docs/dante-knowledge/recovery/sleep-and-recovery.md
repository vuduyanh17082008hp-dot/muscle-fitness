## Recommended sleep duration

Most healthy adults need roughly 7–9 hours of sleep per night. Consistently
sleeping outside this range is associated with worse recovery and health
outcomes, though individual needs vary somewhat (Hirshkowitz M, et al.
"National Sleep Foundation's sleep time duration recommendations:
methodology and results summary." Sleep Health, 2015).

## Recovery is multidimensional

Recovery is multidimensional — physiological, psychological, and social —
and under-recovery relative to training load is a primary driver of
overreaching and overtraining. Monitoring recovery should combine multiple
simple markers (sleep, soreness, stress, mood) rather than relying on a
single number in isolation (Kellmann M, et al. "Recovery and Performance in
Sport: Consensus Statement." International Journal of Sports Physiology
and Performance, 2018).
