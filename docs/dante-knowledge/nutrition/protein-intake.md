## How much protein is enough

Most exercising individuals benefit from roughly 1.4–2.0 g/kg of body mass
per day, distributed across multiple meals, to support muscle protein
synthesis and recovery. Needs can be higher during caloric restriction,
when protein helps preserve lean mass despite a calorie deficit (Jäger R,
Kerksick CM, Campbell BI, et al. "International Society of Sports Nutrition
Position Stand: protein and exercise." Journal of the International
Society of Sports Nutrition, 2017).

## Distribution matters, not just the daily total

Spreading protein intake across multiple meals (rather than one large
serving) is associated with better muscle protein synthesis over the day.
This is a distribution guideline, not a rigid per-meal minimum — the total
daily intake and long-term consistency matter more than hitting an exact
per-meal number every time.
