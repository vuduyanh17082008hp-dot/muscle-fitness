## Efficacy for performance

Creatine monohydrate is one of the most-studied and consistently
effective supplements for improving high-intensity exercise performance
and supporting training adaptations, typically at doses of 3–5 g/day for
maintenance (Kreider RB, et al. "International Society of Sports Nutrition
position stand: safety and efficacy of creatine supplementation in
exercise, sport, and medicine." Journal of the International Society of
Sports Nutrition, 2017).

## Safety profile

At typical doses, creatine monohydrate has a well-established safety
profile in healthy individuals across long-term use in the studied
literature. As with any supplement, a person with an existing kidney
condition or other relevant medical concern should check with a physician
before starting supplementation — general population safety data does not
override individual medical guidance.
