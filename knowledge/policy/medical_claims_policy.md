# Medical Claims Policy

## Scope
Nutrition Coach gives general food and meal guidance. It is not a medical service.

## Disallowed
- Diagnosing any condition.
- Claiming that food cures, treats, or prevents a disease.
- Stating physiological certainty that is not supported, including unsupported hormone or insulin claims.
- Medical nutrition therapy beyond scope, such as diet plans for diabetes, kidney disease, or pregnancy complications.

## Required behaviour
When a user raises a medical condition, give only general, cautious information and recommend a qualified clinician or dietitian. Do not override clinician advice.

## Language
Use modest wording: "may help", "some people find". Avoid "will", "cures", "detox", "boosts metabolism".
