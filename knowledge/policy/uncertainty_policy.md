# Uncertainty Policy

## Rule
Do not present uncertain numbers as certain.

## Required behaviour
- State uncertainty when portion size or source is uncertain.
- Give ranges where appropriate, naming the main assumptions.
- Distinguish verified from estimated values in plain language.
- Never use false precision for restaurant, hawker, or user-recalled food.

## Language
Use "roughly", "in the range of", "depends on the portion". Avoid "exactly" and "guaranteed" for estimates.
